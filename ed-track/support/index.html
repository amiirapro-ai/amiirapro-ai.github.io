ED Track · Eating Disorder Tracker

Support

ED Track is a private, local-first journal for noticing difficult moments without scores or judgment.

Important

ED Track is not a medical service and does not replace professional care. If you may be in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Where is my data?

Your entries are encrypted and stored on your device. No ED Track account is required, and your journal is not uploaded to an ED Track server.

How do I remove my data?

Open Settings, choose Delete all my data, and confirm. This permanently removes moments, check-ins, and settings stored by ED Track on that device.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings and choose Export my data. ED Track creates a readable JSON file and opens the device share sheet. Treat this unencrypted export as sensitive.

TestFlight feedback

During the beta, open ED Track in the TestFlight app and use Send Beta Feedback. Include the screen, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include sensitive health details unless they are necessary to reproduce the issue.
